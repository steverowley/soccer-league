== 1. WHY THE CONTRACT MOVED TO REAL SQUADS

matchDistributions.test.ts played two IDENTICAL 4-4-2 sides rated 60 to 80. Identical sides
cancel each other out and make a poorer match than the league plays. The live league, with real
squads and real managers, was already inside every band its results let us check (figures from
the live database, read by the parent session):
  Season 1, 234 matches: goals 2.99, draws 0.29, home wins 0.385, 0-0 0.06, saves 6.19
  Season 2, first 80 matches: goals 2.94, draws 0.25, home wins 0.425, saves 6.65, penalties 0.21
while the equal-sides fixture reported gaps in saves, goals, draws and penalties (#739, #708).
#765 closed those fixture gaps but took real-squad goals from 3.09 to 4.01 a match. Owner
decision: measure realism on real squads; target bands unchanged.

== 2. THE FIXTURE

src/features/match/logic/calibration/islSquads.json: snapshot of all 32 clubs (513 active
players) from the live league via the read cache on 2026-11-20 (scripts/snapshot-isl-squads.mjs):
five attribute columns, starter flag, personality (entity trait, else the column), manager's
formation, style and game model. islFixture.ts builds each club exactly as the worker does
(toSpatialTeamInput then applyTeamTraits). Schedule: match m is pair m/2 at one ground or the
other (both venues, different seeds 1000 + m); five pairs in six are league fixtures walking
each league's 28 pairings in turn (all four leagues), the sixth a cross-league tie. Over 480
matches every club appears 22 to 40 times; 80 matches are cross-league.
The test plays matches 0..23; `bash scripts/run-batch.sh <prefix> isl 480` plays 0..479, so
every long batch contains the test's batch.

== 5. KEEPER HIGH-BALL FIX: DECISION

The change: a ball that will pass over the keeper's head (a cross or a lofted through ball
dropping in behind him) is read where it first comes back within his reach, or at the line, and
he goes back for it, instead of diving at the spot it sailed over. No new randomness, no rolls.
Rule set by the owner: ship only if real-squad goals stay inside [2.4, 3.0] and no other band
is left. Goals 2.88 to 2.64 (paired -0.24, SE 0.04): inside. 0-0 0.067 to 0.092: inside. Every
metric inside its band on main stays inside; the four already out move by less than their SE.
SHIPPED.
Where the goals went (240 real-squad matches, goals by the last kick before the ball went in):
main 673 goals of which 63 crosses + 3 through balls (and 24 parries); keeper 618 goals of which
23 crosses + 0 through balls (15 parries); goals from shots 499 to 492. So the fix removes goals
the keeper never faced as a shot, not shot goals. (On the equal-sides fixture #765 measured
untouched cross and through-ball goals at 65 of 281 before, 14 of 268 after, with all its changes.)

The old test played one pair and required a regain difference above 0.16; on main seed 4100's

+0.3) unchanged and far clear.
