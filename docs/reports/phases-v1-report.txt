Shape metrics come from replay frames (1 s) through an observer classifier that is
independent of the engine's phase machine (scripts/phase-metrics.ts):
ip_transition / op_transition = first 6 s after a regain / loss; build_up = ball not past
the opponent's first line; final_third = ball within 35 m of the opponent's goal; blocks by
outfield centroid from own goal (high > 60 m, mid 20-60 m, low < 20 m, Bauer et al.).
Columns: share of in/out-of-possession frames (%), mean spell (s), outfield centroid from own
goal, length and width of the outfield (m), back line = 2nd-deepest outfielder from own goal,
behind = outfielders goal-side of the ball. Restart frames are classed by the side with the ball.
