== 1. WHAT WAS HAPPENING ON THE PITCH (main, 120 matches, 2,170 shots)

a) Shots were struck far less accurately than passes. A shot's execution error was three times
   a pass's in direction (0.26 rad base) and four times a lofted ball's in lift (4.0 m/s), so
   the mean direction error of a foot shot was 14 to 15 degrees whatever the range. Of foot
   shots that were not blocked, 33% from 11 to 18 m and 26% from 18 to 25 m reached the frame,
   12% from beyond 25 m, and about half of the long-range misses went over the bar. Keepers
   had almost nothing from distance to save: foot shots from 18 m and beyond gave 0.54 saves
   a match.
b) Every finish was a full drive. From 6 m a striker used the same back-lift and power as for a
   30 m strike, so close-range chances were blazed rather than placed.
c) Keepers read a high ball only where it crossed their own position. A cross or a lofted
   through ball that passed over the keeper's head was "read" at the spot it sailed over, he
   dived there, and the ball dropped in behind him untouched: 65 of 281 goals (23%) were
   crosses and through balls the keeper never touched.

== 2. BEHAVIOUR CHANGES (no new randomness, no outcome rolls)

1. execution.ts KICK_ERROR.shot: { ang 0.26, lift 4.0 } to { ang 0.136, lift 2.0 }. A shot is a
   kick at full power: about 1.6 times a pass's direction error (the speed-accuracy trade-off),
   and the lofted ball's lift error scaled to shot pace times the same 1.6. The shooter's own
   estimate (evaluate.ts estimateShot) reads the same table, so he knows his own accuracy.
2. Placed finish (evaluate.ts PLACED_*, possession.ts, step.ts): inside 18 m the carrier also
   considers placing it with the side of his foot: half the back-lift, 0.75 of the error, 0.75
   of the speed. It is scored like every other option by his own estimate, never forced.
3. keeper.ts readShot: a ball that will pass over the keeper's head is read where it first drops
   back within his reach (or at the line) and he goes back for it, instead of diving at the
   spot it sailed over.

Diagnosis after (112 matches): foot direction error 6 to 7 degrees; unblocked foot shots on
target 63% inside 11 m, 45% from 11 to 18 m, 32% from 18 to 25 m, 29% beyond; crosses and
through balls that beat an untouched keeper fell from 65 of 281 goals to 14 of 268.
